## 1. SOC 2 Type II

Kestrel Insight has completed a SOC 2 Type II examination covering the Security, Availability, and Confidentiality trust services criteria. The most recent report covers the period 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025 and was issued by an independent CPA firm.

The SOC 2 Type II report is available to customers and prospects under NDA through the Kestrel Trust Center.

## 2. ISO/IEC 27001

Kestrel Ridge Software is certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022. The certificate was issued in June 2025 and covers the Kestrel Insight platform and its supporting engineering, operations, and support functions. Surveillance audits are performed annually.

## 3. Privacy Regulations

- **GDPR and UK GDPR:** Kestrel acts as a processor of customer personal data. A Data Processing Addendum (DPA) is available to all customers. International transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.
- **CCPA / CPRA:** Kestrel acts as a service provider and does not sell or share customer personal information.

## 4. Other Frameworks

Kestrel's security program is aligned with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0. This is a self-assessed alignment and not a certification.

## 5. EU AI Act

Kestrel maintains an inventory of its AI systems and assesses new AI features against the EU AI Act. Kestrel Insight is a general-purpose document intelligence tool and is not intended for the high-risk uses listed in Annex III of the EU AI Act. Customers are responsible for assessing their own use cases.

## 6. Requesting Compliance Documents

Customers and prospects can request the SOC 2 Type II report, ISO/IEC 27001 certificate, penetration test summary letter, and completed standard questionnaires through the Kestrel Trust Center. Some documents require a signed NDA.
