## DRAFT - NOT APPROVED FOR CUSTOMER USE

This draft is under review by Security and Legal. Statements below have not been verified and must not be shared with customers.

## 1. Government and Regulated Industries

Kestrel Insight achieved FedRAMP Moderate authorization in 2026 and is listed on the FedRAMP Marketplace.

Kestrel will sign a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with Enterprise customers that process protected health information.

Kestrel Insight is compliant with PCI DSS 4.0 for the processing of cardholder data.

## 2. Penetration Testing

Independent penetration tests are performed semiannually. A bug bounty program is operated through a public platform.
