## 1. Support Channels

Customers can contact Kestrel Support through in-app chat, by email at support@kestrel-insight.example, and through the support portal. Severity 1 issues should be reported through the support portal or by phone for fastest handling.

## 2. Support Plans

- **Standard Support** is included with all editions and is available 08:00 to 20:00, Monday to Friday, in the customer's hosting region time zone.
- **Premium Support** is included with Enterprise Premium and available as an add-on for Enterprise. It provides 24x7 coverage for Severity 1 and Severity 2 issues and a named technical account manager.

## 3. Severity Levels and Response Targets

| Severity | Definition | Initial response target |
|---|---|---|
| Severity 1 | Production down or critical data issue | Within 30 minutes, 24x7 |
| Severity 2 | Major feature impaired, workaround available | Within 4 hours |
| Severity 3 | Minor issue or general question | Within 1 business day |
| Severity 4 | Feature request or documentation | Within 2 business days |

## 4. Releases and Maintenance

Kestrel Insight uses continuous delivery, with production releases typically shipped weekly. Most releases are deployed without downtime. Kestrel gives at least 30 days' notice of changes that materially alter existing functionality.

## 5. API Versioning and Deprecation

Public API versions are supported for at least 12 months after a successor version is released. Deprecations are announced in the release notes and by email to workspace administrators.

## 6. Onboarding and Customer Success

Enterprise and Enterprise Premium customers receive a guided onboarding program and an assigned customer success manager. Administrator training is delivered through live sessions and the Kestrel Academy online course library.
